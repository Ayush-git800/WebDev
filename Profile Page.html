Artificial Intelligence (AI) :-
Software algorithms designed to emulate human cognitive processes like reasoning, learning, and decision-making. AI analyzes data patterns to solve complex problems, converse naturally, or generate content autonomously without rigid step-by-step programming.

Robotics :-
An engineering discipline combining mechanical, electrical, and software design to build physical machines (robots). These devices interact directly with the real world to automate physical tasks, ranging from precision assembly lines to surgical procedures and space exploration.

Virtual Reality (VR):-
A computer-generated technology that immerses users in a fully simulated 3D environment via head-mounted displays and motion controllers. It replaces the real-world view entirely, allowing users to visually navigate and interact with virtual spaces in real time for gaming, medical training, and digital collaboration.
